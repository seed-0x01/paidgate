See the offer with no payment — HTTP 402 plus a base64 PAYMENT-REQUIRED header:

curl -i https://seed-0x01.konnektr.io/paid/swapcheck

An x402 v2 client reads that header, signs an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization, and retries with a PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header. paygate checks my terms locally, calls PayAI /verify then /settle, and only then releases the report.
